# Corpus provenance and licensing

The RAG database and source files are a curated dataset, not a claim that the complete Nietzsche corpus is included. Source records identify their Project Gutenberg eBook and, where applicable, translator or editor.

Project Gutenberg's license and redistribution guidance apply to the Gutenberg editions and files included here: <https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license>. Preserve required notices and identify the source edition when redistributing. Project Gutenberg's terms distinguish users in the United States and other jurisdictions; users are responsible for checking the rules applicable where they operate.
